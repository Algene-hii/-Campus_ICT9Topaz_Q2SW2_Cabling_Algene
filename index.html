O.B. Montessori School uses “Seipsum facit persona” is a Latin phrase that translates to “Man makes himself” the phrase means "Man Makes Himself". The school uses this doctrine to emphasize its core mission: transforming students into independent, self-disciplined, and self-developing individuals who take active responsibility for constructing their own character and future.
